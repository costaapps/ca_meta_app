## Legacy identity and tenant boundary

`IdDatabase` identifies a legacy database context; it is not sufficient tenant identity. The new `tenant_id` means the organization authorized by an authenticated principal and membership decision. That authorization is not established by a browser-supplied `CompanyUUID`, `IdDatabase`, `IdCompany`, or an external asset ID.

Preserve the legacy company identity as the complete pair `(legacy_database_id, legacy_company_id)`, qualified by source system and environment where those are distinct. Preserve both identifiers as opaque strings; do not parse external/provider IDs or legacy IDs into JavaScript numbers. Resolve this tuple only through an internal allowlisted tenant map after server-side authorization. An HTTP parameter, event, or browser state must never select a database name, DSN, host, schema, or credential.

Proposed mapping records:

| Mapping | Required fields | Candidate uniqueness / handling |
|---|---|---|
| `LegacyTenantMap` | `tenant_id`, `legacy_database_id`, `legacy_company_id`, `source_system`, `environment`, `source_version`, `projection_version` | Candidate `UNIQUE(source_system, environment, legacy_database_id, legacy_company_id)`. Whether one tenant may map multiple approved legacy pairs is a contract decision; do not impose reverse uniqueness without evidence. Actual target DDL is future work. |
| `LegacyTenantAlias` | `tenant_id`, `legacy_company_uuid`, `legacy_database_id`, `legacy_company_id`, `source_system`, `environment`, `source_version`, `mapping_status` | Preserve the UUID as a restricted opaque alias only after the full legacy pair resolves to an authorized tenant. Candidate `UNIQUE(source_system, environment, legacy_company_uuid)` must be checked against DDL/data and is not asserted to exist. Missing, duplicate, or pair-mismatched aliases are quarantined. The UUID is not the new `tenant_id` or a database selector. |
| `LegacyAssetMap` | `tenant_id`, `legacy_database_id`, `legacy_company_id`, `source_system`, `environment`, `legacy_table`, `legacy_row_id`, `legacy_parent_row_id`, `provider`, `asset_kind`, `external_asset_id`, `source_version`, `projection_version`, `mapping_status` | Candidate unique source-row identity `(source_system, environment, legacy_database_id, legacy_company_id, legacy_table, legacy_row_id)`; candidate asset identity `(tenant_id, app_config_id, environment, provider, asset_kind, external_asset_id)`. Never use a global `ExternalId` key. |

The exact mapping must also preserve each old `MetaConnectedPages.Id` and `MetaConnectedNumbers.Id`, and the child-to-WABA relation. Include `tenant_id` in business foreign keys and conflict checks. A reused external ID in another tenant or another `Type` remains a separate asset. A collision within the same complete asset key is a conflict requiring review, not an upsert that can change ownership.

Required mapping provenance is `tenant_id`, `legacy_database_id`, `legacy_company_id`, `external_asset_id`, `source_version`, and `projection_version`. Keep legacy IDs and provider IDs as opaque strings. `source_version` is an immutable source snapshot/watermark identifier; `projection_version` orders a target projection for its aggregate and destination. Neither is derived from `ConnectedAt`, login recency, nor a Git commit. These are proposed contract fields, not columns observed in the legacy tables.

### Credentials and grants

Model an authorization grant separately from each stored credential: a proposed `MetaGrant` owns the verified provider/app/environment/subject relationship and grant state, while `MetaCredential` stores a protected secret reference, credential family, optional grant reference, observed-at provenance, and expiry evidence state. The exact target schema is not defined here. Keep legacy `UserAccessToken` and `PageAccessToken` as separate credential families; associate either with a grant only after a trusted provider response or an approved migration mapping proves that relationship. A token's presence, stored value, or shared row is not proof of grant, permissions, asset scope, or current validity. Do not invent permission names or combine credentials across grants. If grant ownership cannot be verified, quarantine the credential reference and require later reauthorization/reconciliation. Raw credential values never enter fixtures or reports.

## Integrity findings and quarantine rules

The integrity measures are not run because no database endpoint, sanitized snapshot, or DDL is available. `integrity-report.md` records each required metric and its status. Do not infer counts from source code.

1. Resolve the approved tenant map using the full legacy pair and authorized organization. A `CompanyUUID` row that is absent, duplicated, maps inconsistently through `Company` and `Databases`, or cannot be authorized is quarantined.
2. Scope all asset identities by tenant, source system/environment, provider, and type. The same `ExternalId` in another company or type must not cause merge or reassignment.
3. A number with no unique `IdWaba` parent, or a parent whose database/company/type does not match the approved map, is quarantined. Verify Instagram `ParentChannelId` resolves to a Facebook parent in the same tenant; a missing or cross-company parent is quarantined. No last-login, callback recency, or `ConnectedAt` fallback is allowed.
4. Preserve source IDs and raw legacy expiry representation in a restricted migration record. Normalize deadline evidence independently: `KNOWN` only when a trustworthy provider response/version establishes a parseable deadline; `NO_DEADLINE_RETURNED` only when a successfully observed provider response is known to omit/explicitly return no deadline for the relevant token family; `UNKNOWN` for missing/unavailable response, unparseable or absent source provenance, or legacy sentinel without provider evidence. Do not interpret SQL `NULL`, a 60-day application fallback, or literal `Never` as proof of no expiry. The callbacks set `ExpiresUserAccessTokenAt` from `expires_in ?? 5184000` and set `ExpiresPageAccessTokenAt` to the literal `Never`; they do not persist why the value was chosen.
5. Reports may show aggregate counts and approved internal row IDs. Credential equality checks must use a protected, keyed HMAC fingerprint created in a restricted process; never print, persist in the report, or export raw tokens, app secrets, OAuth codes, DSNs, or database credentials. Fingerprints are for within-run duplicate detection, not a reusable credential or authorization proof.

## Dry-run classification algorithm

The offline experiment in [`experiments/data/`](../../../experiments/data/m1_02_dry_run.py) implements only the synthetic decision rules. A future database dry-run must first reconcile the actual DDL and `SHOW INDEX` output against the source queries and four upsert occurrences in the query plan; it must stop if that evidence is missing or incompatible. Then:

1. Pin a consistent, immutable source snapshot/watermark and explicit `source_version`, mapping version, and `projection_version`.
2. Resolve every row using the complete approved `(source_system, environment, IdDatabase, IdCompany)` mapping. Validate any `CompanyUUID` only as a restricted alias against that resolved pair and compare the `Databases` alias as a consistency check. Missing, duplicate, unauthorized, or inconsistent pairs/aliases are quarantined; never route from a request or infer tenant from UUID or `IdDatabase` alone.
3. Resolve asset keys within tenant, provider, type, environment, and external ID. Preserve legacy row IDs and parent links. Repeated external IDs across complete tenant keys remain distinct; duplicate rows inside a complete key are conflicts.
4. Resolve every parent to exactly one row of the required type and tenant. Missing, multiple, cross-tenant, or wrong-type parents are quarantined. Do not choose a candidate by latest login, callback time, or `ConnectedAt`.
5. Classify expiry as `KNOWN` only from a trustworthy, parseable, versioned provider observation; as `NO_DEADLINE_RETURNED` only from a successful observation that confirms no applicable deadline for that credential family; otherwise use `UNKNOWN`. Preserve the source sentinel/fallback separately. A literal `Never`, SQL `NULL`, or the `5184000` fallback alone remains `UNKNOWN`.
6. Compute counts, conflicts, and quarantine reason codes in memory; emit aggregate output and approved internal source-row references only. Do not include credentials, raw external IDs, customer text, or untrusted payloads. The synthetic runner has no database, provider, or file-write path.
7. Reconcile counts and conflicts against separately approved read-only results before any later import proposal. No import starts until the real DDL/index and owner gates in the query plan are satisfied.

## Migration, recovery, and gates

Backfill is proposed as a non-writing dry-run followed by an explicitly approved, checkpointed import. Dry-run output includes snapshot/source version, projection version, counts by table/type/status, orphan and duplicate counts, and quarantined source row IDs under restricted access. It writes no production or staging rows, tables, checkpoints, or files. Import must use an immutable source snapshot or recorded watermark, preserve both legacy IDs and parent links, and refuse to overwrite a credential/configuration changed after the snapshot. A write-enabled migration, DDL, rollback, and recovery procedure are out of scope until the real schema, replication, backups, target catalog, and owners are supplied and reviewed.

Current source changes do not prove the actual synchronization direction or other writers. Keep one recorded writer per tenant/cohort/field; resolve conflicts explicitly, and do not silently choose newest timestamps. Secrets are not included in reports and are not projected by default. A credential that cannot be associated with a verified grant remains unavailable/quarantined and requires later authorization/reconciliation, not broad reuse.

Definition of ready for any import: sanitized central and company DDL and `SHOW INDEX` evidence for all six named tables; confirmed source and target catalogs; source snapshot/watermark; inventory of `GeneralSettings` and notification writers; authorized tenant mapping; real unique keys reviewed against every upsert; and an approved read-only run with restricted credential fingerprint handling. Missing evidence is a hard gate for import/cutover, not a reason to guess.
